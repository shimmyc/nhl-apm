What the columns mean
TOI_5v5
Minutes this skater was on the ice at 5-on-5 with both goalies in net. Power plays, 4-on-4, 3-on-3 overtime and empty-net time are all excluded.
Official_PM
The plus-minus the NHL publishes. It counts every situation except power-play goals, so empty-net goals and shorthanded goals land in it.
GD_on
Goals for minus goals against while this skater was on the ice, counting only clean 5-on-5 play.
NonClean
Official_PM minus GD_on: how much of the published number came from somewhere other than 5-on-5 - empty nets, 4-on-4, shorthanded goals. A big number here means the official figure is telling you about special teams, not about this skater's 5-on-5 play.
Expected
The 5-on-5 goal differential an average skater on this team would have posted in the same number of minutes. On a team being outscored this is negative for everyone.
Adjusted
GD_on minus Expected: 5-on-5 goal differential versus what an average Hawk would have in the same minutes. Positive means the team did better with them on the ice than it did on average.
Rel
On-ice differential compared with the same team's differential while this skater was on the bench, scaled by the minutes split.
GD_on/60
GD_on expressed as a rate per 60 minutes of 5-on-5, so short-TOI skaters are comparable with regulars - though a small number of minutes makes the rate jumpy.
