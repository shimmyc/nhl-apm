Every season in the data that passed verification. A traded season shows a TOT row, then one row per club. Rel for a traded season or a career is worked out per club per season and added up. Games that failed verification are left out of their season, so GP can be a game or two below the official count. Age is on Feb 1 of the season's second year. NonClean is Official_PM minus GD_on (goals at other strengths). Team 5v5 GD is the club's own 5-on-5 goal differential over that season's verified games, so good-team and bad-team years show next to his numbers. Per 60 is per 60 minutes of his 5v5 ice time and applies to Rel, GD_on, Expected and Adjusted.
