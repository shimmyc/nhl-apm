My books
Fees
Fee per bet, by book ($)

A line uses the fee of the selected book with the best price (on a tie, the cheaper fee). Min stake = that fee ÷ (EV × max share): the smallest bet at which the fee takes no more than that share of the expected profit. While on, EV and results are net of one fee per bet at your unit, a void included (one per parlay). Paper pick selection ignores it. Kept in this browser.
